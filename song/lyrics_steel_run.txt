[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Cold forge. Dark before the light.
No one claps for building heat.
Hours burn before I strike.
Then the hammer. Then the beat.

[Chorus]
Fold me over. Strike me through.
Hold me up against the day.
Every mark you see is true.
Look — the pattern is the blade.

[Verse 2]
Quench me hard: I come out glass.
Hard enough to break in two.
Back into the lower flame:
Bend me now — I spring back true.

[Chorus]
Fold me over. Strike me through.
Hold me up against the day.
Every mark you see is true.
Look — the pattern is the blade.

[Bridge]
Some days nothing takes the shape.
Some days every hammer missed.
Still the fire. Still the hand.
Still the work. And still I stand.

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 3]
Edge at last. The work is through.
Wrapped in cloth and handed on.
Someone's hand will carry this
Farther than I'll ever go.

[Chorus]
Fold me over. Strike me through.
Hold me up against the day.
Every mark you see is true.
Look — the pattern is the blade.

[Outro]
Count the folds. They are the proof.
Look — the pattern is the blade.
