[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Four a.m. The voice says, "Stay.
Rest — you've more than earned the day."
Feet on the floor, cold as slate:
Not today. The rest can wait.

[Chorus]
Will I quit? Not today.
Will I kneel? Not today.
Come whatever comes my way,
Every answer's "Not today."

[Verse 2]
Legs are lead and lungs are fire.
Every step is one step higher.
All the ones who swore I'd quit —
Not today. Get used to it.

[Chorus]
Will I quit? Not today.
Will I kneel? Not today.
Come whatever comes my way,
Every answer's "Not today."

[Bridge]
Someday something stops us all.
Someday even mountains fall.
Till that morning, I decline:
Not today. Today is mine.

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 3]
Every "not today" I've said,
Every morning left for dead,
Look what all of them became —
Iron will that bears my name.

[Chorus]
Will I quit? Not today.
Will I kneel? Not today.
Come whatever comes my way,
Every answer's "Not today."

[Outro]
Will I quit? Not today.
Come what may — not today.
