[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Cold forge. Dark before the light.
Hands that no one ever sees.
Thousand strikes to get it right.
Nobody counts them all but me.

[Chorus]
Fold me over. Strike me through.
Every fire I walk into
Leaves a line that will not fade.
Look — the pattern is the blade.

[Verse 2]
Quench me hard: I come out glass.
Hard enough to snap in two.
Back into the smaller flame.
Soft enough to carry through.

[Chorus]
Fold me over. Strike me through.
Every fire I walk into
Leaves a line that will not fade.
Look — the pattern is the blade.

[Bridge]
Some days nothing takes the shape.
Some days every hammer missed.
Still the fire. Still the hand.
Still I come back after this.

[Instrumental Break]

[Verse 3]
Edge at last. The work is through.
Not for glory. Not for show.
Someone's hand will carry this
Farther than I'll ever go.

[Chorus]
Fold me over. Strike me through.
Every fire I walk into
Leaves a line that will not fade.
Look — the pattern is the blade.

[Outro]
Count the folds. They are the proof.
Look — the pattern is the blade.
