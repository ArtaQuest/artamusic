[intro]
They made me in the fire.
They beat me on the stone.
They dropped me in the water.
They left me hard as bone.

[verse]
Strike! and the hammer falls.
Strike! and the iron rings.
The coals go white, the smoke goes grey,
each blow takes the rust away.
Strike! I am folded twice.
Strike! I am drawn out thin.
The fire gives me all its heat.
The anvil sets my edges true.

[verse]
Strike! and they lift me high.
Strike! and the water screams.
The steam goes up, the glow runs out,
what comes back is cold and hard.
Strike! I am set to cool.
Strike! I am put to test.
Hard steel breaks and soft steel bends.
The fire showed me where that ends.

[chorus]
I am the weight in your hand.
I am the edge that holds.
I was made inside the fire
and I don't break in the cold.
The fire is out, the smith is gone,
the forge has turned to grey.
Whatever the flame put in me
has never gone away.

[verse]
Strike! when the horn calls out.
Strike! when the fear comes in.
A hand can shake, a heart can turn,
but I stay cold and I stay firm.
Strike! through the broken shield.
Strike! down the darkened field.
The hand picks the road we take.
I only hold the edge I make.

[bridge]
Hard steel breaks.
Soft steel bends.
I was taught
where each one ends.
Hold me tight
and swing me through.
What I keep,
I keep for you.
Bend me far.
Feel me spring.
I come back.
That's the thing.

The next hand lifts me. On we go.
Strike! for the ones who made.
Strike! for the ones who stayed.
They carried me through smoke and rain,
and handed me to you again.
