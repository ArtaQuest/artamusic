[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Strike, and the coals go white.
Fold me over. Hold me tight.
Strike, and the cinders glow.
Let me cool down, long and slow.

[Chorus]
I am the steel you hold.
I don't break in the cold.
I was made inside the flame.
Hold me up and call my name.

[Verse 2]
Lift me high when the drums begin.
Hold me steady. I go in.
Hands grow old and let me fall.
The next hand grips me through it all.

[Chorus]
I am the steel you hold.
I don't break in the cold.
I was made inside the flame.
Hold me up and call my name.

[Bridge]
Hard steel snaps and breaks in two.
Soft steel bends and will not do.
The slower flame taught me how.
Bend me far. I come back now.

[Instrumental Break]

[Chorus]
I am the steel you hold.
I don't break in the cold.
I was made inside the flame.
Hold me up and call my name.

[Outro]
The forge is cold. The smith is gone.
I am the steel. I hold on.

[Instrumental fades out]
