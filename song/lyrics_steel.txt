[intro]
They made me in the fire.
They beat me on the stone.
They dropped me in the water.
They made the edge my own.

[verse]
Strike! and the hammer falls.
Strike! and the iron rings.
The coals go white, the smoke goes grey.
Each blow takes the rust away.
Strike! and the sparks fly wide.
Strike! and the black flakes fall.
The hammer folds me twice and thins.
The hammer beats me out and in.
The fire gives me all its heat.
The anvil makes my edges meet.

[verse]
Strike! and they lift me high.
Strike! and the water screams.
The steam goes up, the glow goes out.
I come back hard. I come back proud.
But hard alone will crack and fly,
so back I go, and not so high:
a low slow heat, a smaller flame,
and I come out not quite the same.

[chorus]
I am the weight in your hand.
I am the edge that holds.
I was made inside the fire
and I don't break in the cold.
The fire is out, the smith is gone,
the forge is cold and grey.
Whatever the flame put in me
has never gone away.

[verse]
Strike! when the horn calls out.
Strike! when the fear comes in.
A hand can shake, a heart can bend.
I hold on to the very end.
Strike! through the broken shield.
Strike! down the darkened field.
The hand decides which road we take.
I hold the edge the fire made.

[bridge]
Hard steel breaks.
Soft steel bends.
The fire taught me
where each one ends.
Hold me tight
and swing me through.
What I keep,
I keep for you.
Bend me far.
Feel me spring.
I come back.
That's the thing.

[verse]
Strike! and the years go by.
Strike! and the young hands grow.
A hand gets old and lets me fall.
The next one lifts me. On we go.
Strike! for the ones who made.
Strike! for the ones who stayed.
They carried me through smoke and rain
and put me in your hand again.

[chorus]
I am the weight in your hand.
I am the edge that holds.
I was made inside the fire
and I don't break in the cold.
The fire is out, the smith is gone,
the forge is cold and grey.
Whatever the flame put in me
has never gone away.

[outro]
The fire is out. The smith is gone.
The forge is cold and bare.
Whatever the flame put in me,
I am still standing here.
