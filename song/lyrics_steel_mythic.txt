[Intro]

[Verse]
Before your name, before your door,
I slept inside the mountain's core.
They drew me burning from the seam —
ten thousand hammers shaped the gleam.

[Chorus]
You name me mercy, name me dread,
the quiet weight beside your bed.
Whatever oath your fathers swore,
I am the steel. I keep the score.

[Verse]
I drank the fire. I took the frost.
I count the hands that held and lost.
Each one grew old and passed me on —
I carry every one now gone.

[Chorus]
You name me mercy, name me dread,
the quiet weight beside your bed.
Whatever oath your fathers swore,
I am the steel. I keep the score.

[Bridge]
Lay me down in barrow ground,
under rain, under stone.
I do not rust. I do not sleep.
I keep what I was shown.

[Chorus]
You name me mercy, name me dread,
the quiet weight beside your bed.
Whatever oath your fathers swore,
I am the steel. I keep the score.

[Outro]
The forge is ash. The line goes on.
Name after name — and I remain.
