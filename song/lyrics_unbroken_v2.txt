v2 (2026-08-01) — the triple-check revision, applied by the review's own lyric surgeon.
Changes from v1 (as sung by takes t1-t4):
  - HOOK: "Unbroken, hold the line" -> "Never broken, hold the line" at all 8 occurrences.
    Rationale (confirmed by an adversarial verifier): an unstressed "un-" pickup is the same
    error class that re-voted "I got able" into "I got eight"; a stressed line-initial "NEV-er"
    cannot be swallowed without deleting a stressed syllable. The TITLE stays "Unbroken".
    DECISION GATE: this change is provisional until the next run's takes are machine-transcribed
    and the hook survives — the same evidence bar every other singability rule met.
  - VERSE 2 rebuilt into four true couplets: away/hate, bill/will, jaw/saw, ladder/hammer
    (keys -> ladder, letter -> notice). "call it hammer" stays section-final; spine untouched.
