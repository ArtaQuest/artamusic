v2 (2026-08-01) — verse 2 rebuilt from a confirmed triple-check finding; hook UNCHANGED.

The review proposed "Never broken" on a mechanism argument (an unstressed "un-" pickup can be
re-voted the way "I got able" became "I got eight"). The change was held PROVISIONAL pending
machine-transcription of real takes — and the evidence came back against it: across t1/t3/t4,
"unbroken" was heard cleanly 22 times with ONE inversion (t3), a ~4% noise rate, not a systematic
failure. By this project's own bar — systemic means failing in every take — the hook stands as
written. The mechanism was plausible; the measurement says no.

What v2 does change (evidence-independent craft, confirmed): verse 2 rebuilt into four true
couplets — away/hate (long-a slant), bill/will, jaw/saw, ladder/hammer (the reference's own
harder/armor feminine-slant shape). keys -> ladder, letter -> notice (an eviction notice is
sharper, and it kills the letter/ladder near-homophone). "call it hammer" stays section-final;
the heat -> hammer -> quench -> edge spine is untouched and now machine-enforced.
